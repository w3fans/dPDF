Support further development

dPDF helps you read and print PDFs, assemble contract pages, prepare visual signatures and stamps, and review documents in separate revisions.

If it makes your daily work easier, you can help fund continued development, testing and improvements. Contributions are voluntary; dPDF remains open source under Apache-2.0.
